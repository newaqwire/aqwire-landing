    8. STORAGE, RETENTION, AND DISPOSAL OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

    How does Aqwire store your information?

    Aqwire stores your data with third-party services that process customer data on behalf of Aqwire, including but not limited to Cloud service providers, billing services such as banks and payment processors, and SaaS providers.

    When we share your personal information/data with third parties, we ensure that the data processed are to be secured and protected under the terms of our Data Sharing Agreement with third parties..

    Our contracts dictate that these service providers only use your information in connection with the services they perform for us and not for their benefit, except as otherwise outlined in this Policy, including without limitation as set forth in the Non-Customer Sales, Service, or Information Inquiries section above.

    We will make reasonable commercial efforts to ensure that they comply with such policy or requirements. However, we will not be liable if any person fails to do so.

    How does Aqwire manage and retain your information?

    Data Retention Policy

    In compliance with BSP Circular No. 1138 on Data Retention, we preserve the full transaction data based on the transaction type.

    • For one-time payments, we retain the data for up to five (5) years based on when the transaction was created.
    • For enrollments or recurring payments, we retain the data for up to five (5) years based on the last payment in the transaction.

    Upon the expiration of such periods, Aqwire will ensure that the personal data under its custody anonymizes the transaction by removing all electronic customer information and then moving it into our archive database for storage in-house using cloud storage servers with sufficient security measures implemented.

    All expired transactions, including the categorized personal information, cannot be accessible anymore, protected from the internal and merchant dashboards unless required by law. This period may extend beyond the end of the relationship with us but only for so long as we are contractually bound to do so, or so far as is necessary for the audit, legal/compliance, or other accounting purposes.

    How does Aqwire dispose your information?

    Data Disposal Policy

    We will retain personal information for as long as you remain an active Aqwire Paid Account Holder and for a reasonable time afterward.
    We have a variety of obligations to retain the data that you provide to us, including to ensure that transactions can be appropriately processed, including but not limited to:

    • Settlement, chargeback, and or refunds
    • Prevent and investigate fraud, illegal or prohibited activities, security issues, and suspicious activities
    • Compliance with Anti-Money Laundering and other applicable laws and rules that apply to us and our financial service providers

    When your data is no longer needed, we have procedures either to destroy, delete, erase or convert it to an anonymous form in a secure manner.

    Such retention beyond the intended processing period is aggregated, de-identified, and encrypted to protect your identity during a personal data breach. Any data that is no longer required by relevant laws or jurisdictions in which we operate, legitimate business purposes, or purposes for which Aqwire collected initially will be securely disposed of.

    The Data Protection Officer and Chief Technology Officer are responsible for reviewing the period in retaining all personal data. They shall identify any data no longer needed in the context of the registered purpose/s.

    Your data shall be destroyed and disposed of within the planned period. It is handled as follows:

    • The disposal must be by a method with no possibility of reconstructing or retrieving the contents.
    • A simple deletion of electronic records does not satisfy destruction.
    • All database backups must be reviewed quarterly to ensure that the deletion schedule is being followed.

    After that, your data is prevented from further processing, disclosure, and unauthorized access to any other party.

    However, there may also be residual data that will remain within our databases and other records, which will not be removed. We reserve the right to retain anonymized and aggregate data indefinitely.

    We comply with all applicable laws and regulations to data processing, such as the Data Privacy Act (DPA) of 2012 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. Our Privacy Policy values and protects your information under the applicable data protection legislation, and your data remains protected to the standards described in this Privacy Policy.
